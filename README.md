# Holiday-Activity

## WorkSOPro - Work Smart, Operate Professionally

A revolutionary productivity and well-being application designed for Apple Inc. that truly makes the company a profit while increasing productivity and well-being.

## What is WorkSOPro?

WorkSOPro is a comprehensive application that combines:
- **Smart Task Management**: AI-powered task allocation and prioritization
- **Team Collaboration Hub**: Centralized communication and project tracking
- **Well-Being Integration**: Wellness prompts, micro-break reminders, and focus tools
- **Data-Driven Insights**: Analytics on productivity, resource allocation, and profit impact
- **Community Chatter Lounge**: Physical and digital employee spaces for communication and winddown
- **[CxT] Roasting Company Integration**: Premium coffee shop experience in the workplace

## Key Features

### 1. Smart Productivity Management
- AI-powered task prioritization
- Focus sessions (Pomodoro technique)
- Goal tracking and analytics
- Project management with team collaboration

### 2. Well-Being Monitoring
- HealthKit integration for comprehensive wellness data
- Smart break reminders (every 90 minutes)
- Stress level tracking
- Screen time management
- Posture alerts via Apple Watch

### 3. Community Chatter - Employee Lounge
Physical lounge features:
- Comfortable seating areas (couches, lounge chairs, collaborative tables)
- Interactive zones (whiteboards, pinboards, digital screens)
- [CxT] Roasting Company Café Corner
- Entertainment options (books, magazines, board games)
- Open, inviting layout with natural lighting and plants

Digital lounge features:
- Multiple themed lounges (General, Winddown, Social, Wellness, Celebration)
- Real-time messaging with reactions
- Winddown activities library (meditation, breathing exercises, stretching)
- Team events and celebrations

### 4. [CxT] Roasting Company Coffee Shop
- Premium coffee and refreshments
- Digital ordering system
- Order status tracking
- Menu categories: Espresso, Coffee, Tea, Cold Brew, Snacks, Pastries
- Integrated into physical Community Chatter lounge

### 5. Advanced Analytics
- Productivity dashboard with trends
- Well-being score calculation
- ROI calculator for business impact
- Team analytics and insights
- Predictive recommendations

## Technology Stack

- **Language**: Swift (iOS, iPadOS, macOS)
- **UI Framework**: SwiftUI
- **Backend**: CloudKit (Apple's native cloud)
- **Health Integration**: HealthKit
- **ML/AI**: Core ML for predictions
- **Payments**: StoreKit for subscriptions
